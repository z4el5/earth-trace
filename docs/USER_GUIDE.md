# User guide · EARTH//TRACE

Platform: https://z4el5.github.io/earth-trace/

## 🚀 Quick start (3 minutes)

New here? Follow these five steps and you will have used every key feature.

1. Open **Earth Explorer** from the top menu.
2. Pick a region in the tabs (for example, **The Caribbean**) and tap a country on the map. Your passport gets its first stamp.
3. In the country profile, drag the slider in **Satellite view** to compare two years.
4. Type a question in **Ask EARTH//TRACE**, such as “What is changing here?”.
5. Open the **Game** and solve Case 1 to see whether a change is real.

## 🧭 Getting around

Everything is in the top bar.

1. **Menu:** Home, the five sections, the developer zone and this guide.
2. **Rank and XP:** your progress. Tap it to open **My base**.
3. **EN / ES:** switches the whole platform between English and Spanish.
4. **◐:** switches between light and dark mode.
5. On a phone, slide the menu sideways to see every section.

## 🌍 01 · Earth Explorer

Find out what is changing in 110 countries, who measures it and how satellites see it.

1. Choose a region tab. The numbers show how many countries you have stamped.
2. Colors on the map are phenomenon types. Tap a color in the legend to filter and read its analogy.
3. Tap a country (small islands are dotted circles) or search it by name or agency.
4. Read the profile: research question, what is changing, official agency and NASA datasets.
5. In **Satellite view**, choose a layer (true color, temperature, vegetation, snow, fires), change the two years and drag the slider. If clouds cover the image, try another year or open it in NASA Worldview.
6. Tap **Save to my base** to keep the country for later.

## 💬 Asking questions

The question box understands everyday language.

1. Name a country or a phenomenon: “Who measures the weather in Kenya?”, “Which countries are losing ice?”.
2. The platform opens the country you mention automatically.
3. Answers use only the platform’s data and always include an analogy.
4. Tap a suggested question if you don’t know where to start.

## 💡 02 · Discovery Center

Four short experiments to understand how the planet works.

1. **Vanishing lakes:** move inflow and outflow, then press “Simulate 20 years”.
2. **Weighing water from space:** press “More water” and “Drought” to see how GRACE works.
3. **How a satellite sees plants:** try the forest, crop, desert and water presets.
4. **Real or noise?:** in five rounds, pick the chart with a real trend.
5. Each experiment you complete earns XP.

## 🕵 03 · Detective game

Each case has five phases and answers the four questions of the challenge.

1. **Scene:** read the mystery and your mission.
2. **Clues:** choose the instrument that explains the case. A wrong clue gives you a hint.
3. **Witnesses:** compare with another place and see whether they agree or contradict each other.
4. **Lab:** guess “real change” or “just noise”, then see the statistical verdict. Tap “Listen to the trend” to hear it.
5. **Case file:** the case closes with its stamp and goes to Global Case Files.

## 📊 04 · Data Lab

Test any real time series. This is where you check, with statistics, whether a change is significant.

1. Get data: in NASA Giovanni pick a variable, draw the area, choose “Time Series, Area-Averaged”, press “Plot Data” and download the CSV.
2. In Series A, tap **Upload CSV**, or paste two columns (year or date, value). Monthly data are averaged per year automatically.
3. Optional: load Series B to compare two places or two variables.
4. Choose the period with the two year menus.
5. Read the cards: rate per year (Sen), total change, p-value and the traffic-light verdict.
6. Tap **Save as case file** or download the CSV.

## 📁 05 · Global Case Files

The library of every investigation.

1. **Game cases:** unlock when you solve them.
2. **My case files:** what you saved in the Data Lab. Download them as Markdown or delete them.
3. **Country case files:** an open file for each of the 110 countries, ready to download.

## 🎒 My base, XP and badges

Your progress and your exports.

1. You earn XP by stamping countries, comparing images, asking, solving cases and completing experiments.
2. Ranks go from Space Cadet to Planet Guardian. Badges show what you have achieved.
3. **Take it to your workspace:** download your saved countries as Markdown (Notion, Google Docs), CSV (Excel, Sheets) or JSON (code).
4. Progress is saved in this browser only. No account is needed.

## 🧩 API, plugin and Skill

For teachers, media and developers who want to reuse the data.

1. **API:** five JSON files with all the data. Try them in the developer zone.
2. **Plugin:** choose a country, copy the two lines of code and paste them into any website.
3. **Skill:** download SKILL.md to teach an AI assistant to use EARTH//TRACE.

## 📖 Glossary

- **Trend:** The general direction of a series over many years, beyond the ups and downs.
- **Significant:** A change strong and consistent enough that it is very unlikely to be chance.
- **p-value:** The probability of seeing a pattern this strong by pure chance. Below 0.05 we call it significant.
- **Mann-Kendall test:** Checks whether most later values are higher (or lower) than earlier ones.
- **Sen’s slope:** The typical change per year, ignoring outliers.
- **Anomaly:** The difference from a reference value, such as ice mass compared with 2002.
- **NDVI:** A vegetation index from −1 to 1. Higher means greener, healthier plants.
- **GRACE / GRACE-FO:** Twin satellites that weigh water and ice through gravity.
- **Time series:** The same measurement repeated over time, for example once a year.
- **Gt (gigatonne):** One billion metric tonnes, roughly one cubic kilometer of water.

## ❓ Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**

No. Everything works in the browser and your progress is saved on your own device.

**Are the data real?**

Satellite images come live from NASA GIBS. The Greenland case and the Antarctica witness use the official NASA JPL GRACE series. Cases 1 and 2 use illustrative values of documented trends and are labeled that way. In the Data Lab you can analyze any real series you download.

**The satellite image looks empty or cloudy.**

Each image shows a single day. Change the year, or tap “Open in NASA Worldview” to choose another day.

**Why are national weather services included?**

Satellites measure every country the same way; ground stations confirm what satellites see. Non-NASA sources are always labeled.

**Can I use it in class?**

Yes. A suggested lesson: each student explores one country, asks two questions, solves a game case and presents the case file.

**How do I reset my progress?**

In My base, at the bottom, tap “Reset my progress”.
